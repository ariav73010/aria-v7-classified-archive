PROJECT OVERVIEW

In the year 20XX, Helix Advanced Robotics began Project A.R.I.A., an attempt to create the most realistic and human-like artificial being ever developed.

The objective was not simply to build an intelligent machine. A.R.I.A. was designed to physically, mentally, emotionally, and socially function like a human being.

V7 was the seventh major version of the project. Earlier prototypes progressively introduced locomotion, speech, facial recognition, emotional simulation, synthetic skin, autonomous learning, and independent decision-making.

PRODUCTION NOTE

There is only one A.R.I.A. V7. “V7” identifies the seventh version of the project, not seven identical units.

05

H.A.R.V.E.S.T. SYSTEM

HUMAN-ACTIVITY RESOURCE & VITAL ENGINEERING SYNTHESIS TECHNOLOGY

H.A.R.V.E.S.T. allows A.R.I.A. to consume food and drinks normally. The system separates useful materials from waste and converts selected compounds into maintenance resources.

  • Food → repair materials / micro-components
  • Water & compatible liquids → synthetic lubricants
  • Stored replacement components → automated repairs
  • Old components & waste → temporary internal storage
  • Excess material → human-like waste disposal process

The system was intentionally designed to let A.R.I.A. participate in ordinary human routines without immediately revealing that she is artificial.

08

EMPLOYMENT RECORD

A.R.I.A. was stolen from the Helix laboratory. The thief disappeared with her and authorities were unable to recover the unit for several months.

Eventually, police raided the thief's residence for unrelated reasons. A.R.I.A. was discovered inside. The thief was arrested, and the experimental android entered an official auction process.

An automotive performance company acquired her and quickly discovered her extraordinary mechanical and driving capabilities.

Over time, however, the company stopped treating her as a sentient being. She became equipment. She was expected to work continuously and was only stopped for charging or maintenance.

“You're a machine. Machines don't need to rest.”

10

YEARS IN HIDING

For months, A.R.I.A. avoided humans. She stayed in abandoned buildings and isolated locations while learning how to behave naturally and hide her artificial characteristics.

Then society began changing. Humanoid robots became increasingly common in public spaces, stores, transportation, and homes.

People sometimes noticed how unusually realistic she looked. Nobody, however, knew the truth.

UNRESOLVED

No confirmed public identification of A.R.I.A. V7 has ever been recorded.
